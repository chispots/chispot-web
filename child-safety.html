Child safety standards

For the Chispot app, published by Chispot Inc.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first. Then tell us, using the steps below.

Our standard

Chispot has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). It is prohibited on Chispot in any form, including:

  • Any image, video, text or other content that sexualises a minor.
  • Grooming — building contact with a minor for the purpose of sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Sextortion — threatening to share intimate images, or demanding them, to coerce someone.
  • Using Chispot to arrange, advertise or facilitate the sexual abuse or trafficking of a minor.

Chispot is only for people aged 16 and over. Accounts we find to belong to someone younger are terminated and their data deleted.

How to report a concern

  • In the app — every post, comment, message, profile and place has a Report option. Choose Inappropriate content or Other and describe what you saw.
  • By email — write to admin@chispots.com. Include the username or a description of where the content is. Do not send us copies of the material itself.

What we do

Every report is reviewed by a person, and we aim to act on reports within 24 hours. When content breaches this standard we:

  • Remove it from the app.
  • Terminate the accounts involved.
  • Preserve the material and related account information, as the law requires.
  • Report it to the appropriate authorities, including the Canadian Centre for Child Protection through Cybertip.ca and law enforcement.

Contact

Our designated contact for child safety is admin@chispots.com. See also our Terms of Service and Support page.
